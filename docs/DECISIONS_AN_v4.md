# DECISIONS_AN_v4 (2026-11-03)

Continues DECISIONS_AN_v3 (#1-12). Earlier files are not edited.

1. **2019 boreal and tropical MCFs.** I added all 10 Table 10.17 (Updated) columns from 2019 Vol 4 Ch 10, p. 10.68 to `data/ipcc_params_AN_v9.csv`. That covers lagoon plus liquid/slurry and pit at 1, 3, 4, 6 and 12 months. Every row is VERIFIED from the PDF text. Tropical is split into its four sub-zones (montane, wet, moist, dry) exactly as the table gives them. For solid storage, dry lot and daily spread, the tropical zones map to the "warm" group, as the table's 3-group header does. Boreal maps to "cool". `ipcc2019/manure.py` `ZONES_2019` now includes all of them.
2. **Moist/dry split: adopt the IPCC 2019 climate-zone map.** I sampled Lewis (2022), Zenodo doi:10.5281/zenodo.7303808, at the county internal point. That map recreates the 2019 Annex 3A.5 decision tree using CRU Penman-Monteith PET, frost days and elevation. It is the most defensible reference because it is the IPCC's own tree with an FAO-56 PET. Hargreaves and Thornthwaite stay as sensitivity columns in `data/county_climate_zones_AN_v1.csv`.
   - **Hargreaves (the AN_v1 rule):** 354 counties are dry under Hargreaves but moist on the map, and 78 go the other way. That is 432 of 2,664 counties with MAT ≤ 18 °C, which confirms the dry lean.
   - **Thornthwaite:** 778 counties go wet-wrong and 1 goes dry-wrong, so Thornthwaite over-corrects. It is not adopted.
   - **Tropical counties:** Adopting the map also resolves the 478 counties with MAT > 18 °C that the nClimDiv rules could not place (they lack frost days and elevation). The map puts 62 counties in tropical moist and 30 in tropical dry. None fall in tropical wet or montane.
3. **Polar counties (4, Alaska).** Table 10.17 has no polar column, so for 2019 these counties use the 2006 per-°C MCFs, flagged `polar->2006_MCF`. For wet or dry they use the moisture of their map class.
4. **2006 MCFs at MAT resolution.** I added the per-°C rows from 2006 Table 10.17 (pp. 10.44–10.45, the same values as Annex 10A) for 10–28 °C. MAT is rounded, clipped to the range 10–28 and looked up per county. Pit over 1 month equals liquid/slurry (Table 10A-7).
5. **Liquid systems use 12-month storage in 2019** (project choice, unchanged). Footnote 1 says to use the 6-month row if retention is unknown. Liquid uses the without-crust MCF and EF3 in both versions.
6. **The same activity data and VS/TAM go into both versions.** These are the 2006 Annex 10A North America values. The 2019 VS defaults (Table 10.13A) are not registered, so the version difference comes from the EFs, B0, MCFs, Nrate, EF1/EF3/EF4/EF5 and Frac factors. Manure system shares are 2006 Annex 10A North America (`data/mms_north_america_ipcc2006_AN_v1.csv`).
   - Poultry: layers are 100% without litter, and broilers and turkeys are 100% with litter. This is an assumption; Table 10A-9 gives no North America split.
   - Swine: all hogs are treated as market swine. The 2022 Census has no county or state breeding-herd inventory, and I have not invented a share. This overstates VS and understates N for the breeding fraction.
7. **(D) withheld census cells.** The primary outputs leave them as NaN, with no imputation, and flag them in `census_flags`. (Z) is treated as 0 and flagged. As a sensitivity, national totals only, each state's withheld residual is allocated to its (D) counties: by cattle inventory for dairy, and in equal shares otherwise. If milk cows are (D), other cattle cannot be derived as total minus milk, so the county loses all cattle. That is the main reason the primary totals are low.
8. **Synthetic fertilizer N.** I used 2017 AAPFCO state N purchased, from the EPA ROE indicator page (the latest year it gives). I allocated it to counties by their share of 2022 Census fertilizer expense, which includes lime and soil conditioners. This mixes years and is an expense proxy, and is documented as a gap.
9. **Soil N2O scope.** It covers F_SN, managed manure N applied and PRP N only. Applied manure N is N in storage × (1 − FracGasMS − FracLeachMS); 2006 has no FracLeachMS. Crop residues, F_SOM, organic soils, sewage sludge and sheep, goats and horses are excluded. Two 2006 FracGasMS cells are not published: other-cattle liquid and swine dry lot. Their indirect N2O is treated as 0 and listed in `param_gaps`.
10. **Wet vs dry** for EF1, EF3PRP, EF4 and FracLEACH comes from the adopted zone, with moist or wet counted as wet. 2006 applies FracLEACH 0.3 only in wet counties.
11. **GWP is AR5** (CH4 28, N2O 265) to match the EPA 2025 edition. The EPA comparison is a consistency check, not validation.
