# DECISIONS_AN_v27 - Soybean-year carry-over status

Carries forward DECISIONS_AN_v26.

- **D27.1 Carry-over is a model result contradicted by field data (author decision, 4 Nov 2026).** The APSIM soybean-year share of fertiliser-induced N2O (0.30-0.51) is reported as a model result that field data contradict: Fabrizzi et al. 2024 (doi:10.1002/jeq2.20566) found no effect of the previous corn's N on soybean-phase N2O, and Parkin & Kaspar 2006 (doi:10.2134/jeq2005.0183) measured soybean-year emissions well below corn years. Diagnosis in FINDINGS_AN_v10: 72-85% of it is residual nitrate denitrified in Jan-Apr after the maize.
- **D27.2 LITCHECK caveats carried into the manuscript (v19 / SI v11).** Rain-pulse shares are a lower bound because freeze-thaw is missed; the 0 N drainage increase is flagged as a possible artifact; the Holos texture effect is a step, not the smooth effect seen in the field.
